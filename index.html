    Photograph a page of music and Claude writes it out as notes the app can play. Check the notation against your photo, fix anything it misread, then play along with a violin sound and a metronome at your own tempo.

    Edit the notes as text (ABC notation)

    Sharp, flat or in tune?

    0 of 0

    You hear a reference note, then a second note. Say whether the second one is sharp, flat or exactly the same. Get it right and the gap shrinks. Get it wrong and it widens, so the drill settles at the smallest difference you can reliably hear.

    40¢
    current gap
    Experienced string players can hear about 5 to 10 cents. Most beginners start around 25 to 40.
