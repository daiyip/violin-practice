# Violin learning tool: feature brainstorm

Assumes a beginner-to-intermediate learner practicing mostly alone, with or without a teacher.
**Quick win** = buildable in a weekend or two with browser APIs. **Ambitious** = real audio/ML work or lots of content.

## 1. Practice tools
- **Tuner** with A=440/442 option and open-string reference tones. *Quick win*
- **Metronome** with accent patterns, subdivisions, and a "speed trainer" that nudges tempo up after clean reps. *Quick win*
- **Drone generator** (sustained tonic/fifth) for intonation practice in any key. *Quick win*
- **Practice timer / session planner**: split a session into warm-up, scales, études, repertoire, with gentle prompts to switch. *Quick win*
- **Loop & slow-down player**: load a recording, loop a passage, slow it down without changing pitch. *Medium*
- **Play-along backing tracks** for scales and simple pieces. *Medium*

## 2. Feedback and analysis (the hard, high-value part)
- **Live pitch meter** that shows cents sharp/flat as you play, not just when tuning. *Medium*
- **Intonation report** after playing a scale: which notes drift, and in which direction (e.g. "your 3rd finger on A string runs sharp"). *Ambitious*
- **Rhythm accuracy** against the metronome: rushing vs dragging. *Ambitious*
- **Tone quality hints** from the audio (scratchy, airy, uneven bow pressure). *Ambitious*
- **Posture / bow-path check** via webcam: straight bow, bow contact point, wrist and elbow position. *Ambitious*
- **Record & compare**: save takes, listen back, A/B against last week or a reference recording. *Quick win to medium*

## 3. Learning content
- **Fingerboard map**: tap a note, see where it lives in 1st/3rd position; quiz mode. *Quick win*
- **Note-reading and ear-training drills** (name the note, sing/play back an interval). *Quick win to medium*
- **Scale and arpeggio library** with fingerings, organized by level (e.g. ABRSM/Suzuki-style progression). *Medium*
- **Technique cards**: short explainers for bow holds, string crossings, shifting, vibrato, with "what good looks like". *Medium*
- **Sheet music viewer** with a follow-along cursor that tracks where you are by listening. *Ambitious*

## 4. Progress tracking
- **Practice log**: minutes, what you worked on, a one-line note on how it felt. *Quick win*
- **Streaks and weekly summary**. *Quick win*
- **Repertoire tracker**: pieces as learning → polishing → performance-ready, with target tempo vs current tempo. *Quick win*
- **Skill progress charts** fed by the analysis features (intonation accuracy, max clean tempo over time). *Medium, depends on section 2*
- **Teacher handoff**: export a week's log and recordings to share before a lesson. *Quick win*

## 5. Smart / AI extras
- **Daily practice plan generator** based on goals, recent logs, and weak spots. *Medium*
- **Ask-a-teacher chat** about technique, theory, or a specific passage. *Medium*
- **Spaced repetition** for passages and drills you keep struggling with. *Medium*

## A sensible first slice
Tuner + metronome + drone + practice log + record-and-playback. All browser-only, useful from day one, and the recordings become the raw material for the ambitious feedback features later.
